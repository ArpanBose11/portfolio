Business Problem

In every deal, someone has to go first.

Pay first and the buyer carries the risk. Deliver first and the seller carries it. Traditional escrow adds a trusted middleman, but the record still lives in one system.

⚠

Counterparty risk

One side must expose itself before the other commits.

▣

Single point of trust

An intermediary becomes the authority over the transaction record.

✎

Silent changes

A database can accept an edited value without preserving the old state.

⌛

Payment deadlock

Disputes or deliberate silence can leave funds stuck without clear execution rules.

Solution

Turn the agreement into executable rules.

Milestone escrow without a single source of truth.

The contract defines who can trigger payment, what happens when work is disputed, and what happens when an approval window expires. The network keeps independent copies of the same history.

Why it works

Three controls make fraud visible.

01

Cryptographic history

Each block has a SHA-256 fingerprint and links to the previous block. Change one record and downstream history no longer matches.

02

Independent copies

Four validator nodes hold the chain. A changed copy can be rejected when it disagrees with the majority.

03

Rules, not discretion

Approval, dispute and seven-day silence rules determine when money can move, rather than relying on an administrator to decide.

Takeaway
